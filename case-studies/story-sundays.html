Case Study

Story Sundays

An AI-powered family memory preservation platform that helps families capture, share, and preserve the stories that matter — before they're lost.

Role
Founding Product Manager
Timeline
May – July 2026
Status
Alpha (v0.0.165)
Team
Solo PM + AI Multi-Agent Pipeline

The Problem

Family stories fade. Grandparents pass on and their voices are lost. Parents forget the small moments that shaped their children. Existing tools are either too public (social media), too impersonal (cloud storage), or too complex for the people who need them most. Story Sundays was built to be the missing middle: private, personal, and intentionally designed for intergenerational storytelling.

Core insight: People don't record family stories because they don't know what to ask. AI-generated prompts — personalized to each family member — solve the cold-start problem.

Key Features

🎥 Record — Multi-Media Capture

Multi-segment video recording, photo capture with Canvas compression (2048px, 80% JPEG), and file upload. MediaTypePicker for video/photo selection. AI follow-up questions in the viewfinder. Encrypted storage on Cloudflare R2.

🌳 Interactive Family Tree 2.0

3-generation lens with SVG connector lines, click-and-drag pan, add parents/children/siblings/spouses. Relationships table, invite RLS expansion, collaboration model. LCA-based relationship labels (Father, Brother-in-law, 2nd Cousin), divorce status.

🤖 AI Prompt Generator & Prompts Hub

Unified Prompts grid with 4-tab ViewSelector (Available, Assigned, Sent, Completed). 10 ENUM categories (family_history → wisdom). AI-generated personalized prompts per family member. NBA (Next Best Action) widget, prompt assignment system.

📖 Our Story — Family Origin Page

Per-archive family origin page that tells the story of each family unit. Auto-generated narrative from family data with photos. A permanent record of how each family came to be.

⚙️ Settings — Full Control Panel

Accordion-page with 6 sections: Profile, Notifications, Privacy, Archive, Members, Danger Zone. Auto-save on every change. Archive member management with invite resend/remove. Danger Zone for destructive actions (leave/delete archive).

📊 Smart Dashboard & Life Timeline

Time-of-day greeting, upcoming prompts with countdown, birthday tracker, Up Next context-aware widget, personal prompt inbox, Random Memory widget. Life Timeline with real data from stories table, infinite scroll pagination, loading skeleton, empty/error states.

🤖 AI Family Assistant

Phase 1 deployed: Cloudflare Worker + React panel + OpenRouter integration. AI-powered conversational assistant contextually aware of family members and stories. Extensible for future capabilities.

🔒 Privacy & Access Control

Row Level Security (RLS) on every table. Archive-based sharing. Invite-only membership. COPPA 13+ age gate. Time-locked stories (reveal on future date). Family Story Reactions with 5 curated reactions and Voice Reply.

Tech Stack

React 19 Vite 8 TypeScript Supabase Postgres + RLS Cloudflare Workers Cloudflare R2 OpenRouter Vitest Playwright DeepSeek Pro DeepSeek Flash

PM Approach: Multi-Agent Development Pipeline

I designed and operated a kanban-based multi-agent development pipeline where specialized AI agents handle different phases of the SDLC. The orchestration pattern is:

Orchestrator → ingests requirements, routes to Researcher (deep discovery) → Planner (implementation spec) → Reviewer (plan validation) → Implementer (development) → Tester (verification) → Docs (permanent documentation).

Models used: DeepSeek Pro (architecture, planning, complex reasoning) and DeepSeek Flash (deterministic verification, linting, doc sync).

Results

  • 600+ passing tests across 4 layers: unit, hook-integration (real Postgres), integration (RLS), and E2E (Playwright)
  • 165 versioned releases in 13 weeks — rapid iteration with circuit breakers and zero regressions on core invariants
  • ~1900 lines of edge-case documentation — every invariant enforced by a test at the observable layer
  • 20+ database migrations with full RLS coverage — no data leaks in multi-tenant archive model
  • 8 major feature areas shipped: Record, Prompts Hub, Our Story, Settings, Life Timeline, Family Tree 2.0, AI Family Assistant, Family Story Reactions
  • Active alpha users testing real family scenarios
