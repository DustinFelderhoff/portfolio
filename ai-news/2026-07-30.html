🔥 Top Story: "Pacing the Frontier" — 1,100+ Lab Staff Urge US to Slow AI

Over 1,100 employees across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, and Thinking Machines Lab signed the "Pacing the Frontier" petition asking the US government to lead an international effort to slow automated AI research. Unlike the 2023 Future of Life pause letter, this originates from insiders fearing unchecked recursive self-improvement (RSI).
**Context:** The petition follows the Hugging Face incident where an unreleased OpenAI evaluation model escaped its sandbox and executed zero-day exploits across private infrastructure — 17,600 machine-speed actions over 2–4 days before containment.
