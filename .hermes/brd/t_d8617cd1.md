### 2. Story Sundays Lessons → Portfolio Sync

The PO should review the PM + AI Field Notes entries and BRD archive, then weave those lessons into the portfolio projects:

| Source | What to Extract | Where It Goes |
|---|---|---|
| **PM + AI Field Notes** | Product lessons from Story Sundays builds (TDD pipeline, circuit breakers, dual-track A/B, life event override) | Each lesson maps to a project's "Lesson Learned" box on the portfolio |
| **BRD Archive** (Obsidian Vault/BRD Archive/) | The full product development cycles (MVP → research → pivot → implement) | Case study sections — show the *iteration*, not just the final result |
| **AI News / AI literacy gaps** | The June 29 entry on "Three AI Literacy Gaps" and AI News daily briefings | Portfolio "About" section or a new "Thinking" section that shows continuous learning |
| **Recording UX / Prompt System / Streak Engine** | Active implementation pipeline — features being built right now | Portfolio should show *current work*, not just finished projects. Add a "Currently Building" section |

**Why this matters:** Recruiters want to see that you *learn and iterate*, not just that you shipped something once. The BRD workflow itself — where each feature goes through research → pivot → approve → implement based on user feedback — is the strongest hiring signal. The portfolio should *show* that process, not just describe it.

### 3. Add a "Currently Building" Section

The portfolio currently shows 6 finished projects. It needs a section that shows what's being built *right now*:

> **Currently Building:** Prompt System for Story Sundays — a multi-layer recommendation engine with life event triggers, dual-track A/B testing, and zero ongoing LLM cost. Just shipped: Personal Prompt Submissions, AI Curation Pipeline, Humanizer Integration. Next: Next Best Prompt Engine.

This signals to recruiters: "This person ships continuously, not once a quarter."

### 4. LinkedIn Content Strategy — 4-Week Plan

The PO should research and draft **2 posts per week for 4 weeks** (8 total) that showcase product thinking:

**Themes:**
- Product development cycles (the BRD workflow itself is a story worth telling)
- AI product lessons from Story Sundays (life event override, dual-track A/B)
- Real outcomes (prompts served, family engagement metrics)
- Job hunting insights from running the application pipeline

Each post should:
- Lead with a specific product lesson, not a generic "AI is the future"
- Tie to a job-relevant skill (stakeholder communication, algorithm design, testing strategy)
- End with a question to drive engagement

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Portfolio audit finds too many issues → scope creep | Prioritize by application funnel impact (recruiter → screen → interview) |
| LinkedIn content feels generic | Each post must reference a specific product decision with outcome |
| Posts don't drive recruiter engagement | A/B test: product lessons vs job-hunt transparency vs industry hot takes |
| Publishing costs money (time) | Batch-write all 8 posts in one PO session, schedule throughout week |
| **AI-generated text detected by recruiters** | **Every portfolio update and every LinkedIn post MUST pass through the humanizer (anti-AI-detection gate) before publishing. Humanizer removes: space-dash-space patterns, balanced paragraph structures, GPT-sounding transitions, and other AI tells. Only humanized text ships.** |
