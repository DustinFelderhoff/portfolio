About Me

Not a bullet-point bio. Here's the short version.

I've been a product manager for 13 years across SaaS, AI, and enterprise. The thread that connects everything is building tools that let other people do their best work. Started in manufacturing, designing production lines where operators could produce great output without fighting the system. That same instinct drives every product I've touched since.

Right now I'm deep in agentic AI. I built one of the first ChatGPT enterprise workflows in 2022, a full-stack AI storytelling platform on my own time, and a multi-agent orchestration system. The projects change, but the question stays the same.

Outside work: new dad. Midwestern family in Texas, wife's family in Florida. Story Sundays exists because I want my son to hear his grandmother's voice when he's older. Not just the words on a screen, but how she told a story.
