What I'm Building Toward

I want to work at the intersection of healthcare and agentic AI, helping people improve their lives through products that actually ship and actually get used.

At Nymbl Science I watched a retirement facility resident who couldn't walk to the dining room alone make it there on her own after using the program. It's still the most joy I've gotten from a job, and it left me with a question I keep coming back to: how many years did I add to this patient's life, and how many of those years did they actually get to enjoy? That's the metric that matters to me.

I'm less interested in inheriting a roadmap than in finding where a team is behind and closing that gap.
